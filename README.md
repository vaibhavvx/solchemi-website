# SOLCHEMI (सोल्केमी) — Café & Restaurant Website
### Shahpur Jat, Siri Fort, New Delhi

A bespoke, editorial, modern digital home crafted for **Solchemi** — an artistic, warm café and bookstore nook in Shahpur Jat, New Delhi.

---

## ☕ Visual & Brand Direction

- **Brand Aesthetic**: Delhi art café + cozy bookstore corner + modern Indian comfort + editorial typography.
- **Palette**:
  - Warm Cream & Ivory (`#FAF7F2`, `#FFFDF9`)
  - Earthy Beige & Sandstone (`#EFE8DC`, `#E5DCCB`)

1. **Full-screen Atmospheric Hero**:
   - High-definition natural light photography of the café.
   - Dual branding: English `SOLCHEMI` & Hindi `सोल्केमी`.
   - Core philosophy: *“Good food. Slow moments. Stay a little longer.”*

   - Story narrative: *“A place to pause.”*

6. **The Solchemi Experience**:
   - 4 numbered architectural cards: *01 Fresh & Wholesome*, *02 A Space to Slow Down*, *03 Cozy & Creative*, *04 Warm Hospitality*.
7. **Café Atmosphere**:
   - Night and evening ambiance showcase with subtle parallax depth.
8. **Instagram-Style Masonry Gallery & Lightbox**:
   - Filterable photo masonry grid.
   - Interactive Lightbox with keyboard arrow navigation and caption display.
9. **Guest Reviews**:
   - Real customer voices from Google reviews with verified reviewer avatars and star ratings.
